VO is your recorded script. It sets the timing for everything.
B-roll covers the VO. Its own audio is your nat sound.
Sound bite: one complete thought, 8 to 12 seconds.
Lower third: name and title, on screen for 4 seconds.

The VO pauses while the bite plays. Nothing plays under the bite.

Before you open Clipchamp

Have these ready

Your VORecorded from your approved script, read out loud and timed. You can also record it in Clipchamp in Step 2.
Your sound biteThe interview clip, plus the speaker's name and title as you confirmed them with that person.
Your b-rollWide, medium and tight shots of real action. Steady, in focus, held.
HeadphonesYou can't judge audio levels through laptop speakers.
